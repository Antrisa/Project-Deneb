  Using it needs a bed stop in tools/build.py SCHEDULES and "sleep d" in ACTION_ROWS.

How it is built (tools/)
- fix_eyes.py: redraws a resident's eyes, brows and blush in the house style.
- rig_parts.py: cuts the side view into a body and an arm (art/rig/npc_<id>_side.png).
- rig_walk.py: builds the side walk from those parts.
- rig_front.py: builds the front and back walks from the standing frames.
- poses.py: the shared pose system. Each activity is defined once (hand targets per frame,
  props: bowl, spoon, food, herb, watering can, water drops, pan, morsel, spatula, rag, glass, bottle, pour, hammer, plank, pad, pencil, valve wheel, crayon, paper, ball, ledger, mug, broom, toy, hand-held game, blade, scanner) and drawn for any rigged
  resident in all four directions, with arms in the resident's own sleeve and skin colours
  and the head from the standing frame. Right is the mirror of left.
Order: fix_eyes.py, rig_parts.py, rig_walk.py, rig_front.py, poses.py.
