# Part 1 — Jasper & Evarra

**Characters:** Jasper (Ervani Head Chef) & Evarra (Vuultam Chief Baker & Gardener)

**Arc Theme:** Found family across species lines, turning scarcity into celebration, and learning that great hospitality comes from heart rather than fancy ingredients.

## 2-Heart Event: "Haute Cuisine on a Ration Budget"

**Trigger:** Enter the Mess Hall in the Central Plaza between 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM with both Jasper and Evarra at 2 Hearts.

**Setting:** The Mess Hall kitchen. Smoke curls gently from a simmering pot while a holo-projector plays a crackling 21st-century video of a Parisian bistro.

- **[Visual]:** Jasper is furiously whisking a grey paste in a steel bowl, his fox ears flattened in concentration. Evarra stands beside him snipping tiny green leaves from a single potted herb box onto a tray of baked ration biscuits.
- **JASPER:** "Non, non, non! Look at the viscosity, Evarra! In the old Earth holos, Chef Laurent's *béchamel* flows like liquid silk! Mine looks like Tikka's hull sealant!"
- **EVARRA:** *(Smiling warmly, her purple-blue cheek blotches shifting softly)* "That might be because Chef Laurent had butter, cream, and flour, Jasper—and we have dehydrated soy-paste and reclaimed station water."
- **JASPER:** *(Spotting the player)* "Ah, mon ami! Tell me honestly—does this smell like a candlelit terrace overlooking the Seine, or does it smell like boiled cardboard?"
- **PLAYER CHOICE:**
  - *Option A:* "It smells... ambitious. Maybe add some of Evarra's herbs?" (+Friendship with both)
  - *Option B:* "Honestly, it looks a little like hull sealant." (+Friendship with Evarra, Jasper laughs ruefully)
  - *Option C:* "It's the passion behind the dish that counts." (+Friendship with Jasper)
- **EVARRA:** "See? Even our newest neighbor agrees. You don't need Earth butter to make people feel at home, little brother."
- **JASPER:** *(Ears perking up)* "Little brother?! Evarra, we were found in the same escape pod—we have no idea who hatched or was born first! Besides, one day I *will* serve a true Parisian feast right here on Echo Landing. You'll see!"

## 4-Heart Event: "The Escape Pod Memory"

**Trigger:** Visit the Top-Deck Observation Lounge after 7:00 PM with both Jasper and Evarra at 4 Hearts. (Before the East Agridome is restored, Jasper brings herb tea and ration-flour tarts instead of honey tarts.)

**Setting:** The Observation Lounge dome. Starlight illuminates Evarra sitting quietly by the viewport with a small, tarnished metal locket.

- **[Visual]:** The player approaches Evarra. She looks up with a gentle smile and pats the bench beside her.
- **EVARRA:** "Do you ever look out at the Neutral Zone and wonder where you were meant to land? People always do a double-take when Jasper and I call each other brother and sister. An Ervani and a Vuultam—anyone with a biology chart can tell we aren't blood."
- **EVARRA:** "S.A.R.A. told us how we got here. A drifting lifepod during a border skirmish about twenty years ago—two infants wrapped in the same thermal blanket, holding onto each other's hands. No registry, no homeworld coordinates. Just each other."
- **[Visual]:** Footsteps approach—Jasper arrives carrying two steaming mugs of herbal tea and a tiny plate of fresh honey-glazed tarts made from the Agridome's first harvest.
- **JASPER:** "I knew I'd find you star-gazing. Look—Merrow let me harvest the first Nectar-Blossoms from the Agridome. Taste this."
- **EVARRA:** *(Takes a bite, eyes lighting up)* "Jasper... it tastes like real sunlight. Like a meadow I've only ever seen in dreams."
- **JASPER:** *(Softly, dropping the theatrical accent)* "Wherever that pod came from, Evarra, we landed in the exact right place. Now come on—if we don't plant those wildflower seeds before morning, you'll never get your bees."

## 6-Heart Event: "Operation Honeybee"

**Trigger:** Enter the East Agridome on a sunny morning with both Evarra and Jasper at 6 Hearts, once the East Agridome is restored.

**Setting:** The blooming Agridome garden. Rows of Star-Tomatoes, Moon-Wheat, and flowering shrubs surround a newly built wooden apiary box crafted by Tobbin.

- **[Visual]:** Evarra kneels beside a glass-walled transport hive delivered by Eshu's mail skiff. Jasper, Merrow, and the three station kids (Pip, Nub, and Kit) watch nervously from a few paces back.
- **JASPER:** *(Wearing an oversized welding helmet from Tikka's shop)* "Are you certain these Earth honeybees recognize the Landing Accord, Evarra? They look remarkably armed!"
- **EVARRA:** *(Laughing softly)* "Take off that silly helmet, Jasper—you're scaring them. Bees only sting when they're frightened. Watch."
- **[Visual]:** Evarra unlatches the hive gate. A soft golden hum fills the Agridome as dozens of fuzzy lunar-adapted honeybees drift out, circling the flowering Nectar-Blossoms before settling peacefully onto Evarra's outstretched hand.
- **PIP:** "Whoa... they're tiny starships!"
- **EVARRA:** *(Turning to the player with tears of joy)* "All my life I dreamed of a garden with flowers and bees. Thank you for helping wake this dome back up. Today, Echo Landing finally feels alive."
- **Gameplay Unlock:** Unlocks Wildflower Honey production. (The Honey-Lavender Brioche recipe moved to Evarra's own 6-heart event.)

## 8-Heart Event: "The Critic's Practice Run"

**Trigger:** Enter the Mess Hall in the evening with both Jasper and Evarra at 8 Hearts, once the hotel is open.

**Setting:** The Mess Hall has been transformed with white linens from Sesska, warm lanterns from Tobbin, and polished glassware.

- **[Visual]:** Jasper is pacing frantically in front of a pristine stove while S.A.R.A.'s mobile holo-emitter and Rusk sit at a corner table acting as test judges.
- **JASPER:** "The Spring Hotel Inspection is weeks away! If Vaelen writes that Echo Landing serves 'frontier gruel,' S.A.R.A.'s five-star dream is ruined! Everything must be authentic Parisian perfection!"
- **S.A.R.A.:** "Calm your whiskers, Jasper. True Parisian hospitality is not rigid imitation—it is celebrating the finest ingredients of the region you inhabit."
- **PLAYER CHOICE:**
  - *Option A:* "Blend French technique with our local lunar crops and honey." (Unlocks signature fusion dish!)
  - *Option B:* "Stick strictly to the Earth holo-recipe—you've practiced it a hundred times."
- **JASPER:** *(Pauses, looking from Evarra's honey jars to the fresh cavern truffles on the counter)* "You're right... Why pretend we're on Earth when we have flavors no Parisian chef has ever dreamed of? Evarra—pass me the cavern truffles and the wildflower honey!"
- **Gameplay Unlock:** Unlocks Jasper's signature dish, *Starlight Soufflé & Lunar Truffle Glaze*—giving a massive score bonus during the annual Spring Hotel Inspection.

## 10-Heart Event: "A Table for the Whole Sector"

**Trigger:** Automatic evening cutscene at the Mess Hall / Central Plaza with both Jasper and Evarra at 8 Hearts, after The Critic's Practice Run.

**Setting:** A starlit community banquet around the Concourse Fountain. Every resident—Terran, Zaa'sh, Vuultam, Moogli, Ervani, Scyldar, Arachnay, and S.A.R.A.—shares a long table laden with fresh bread, pastries, roasted roots, and sparkling cider.

- **[Visual]:** Jasper rings a silver bell while Evarra brings out a towering honey-and-berry cake decorated with edible star-flowers.
- **JASPER:** *(Raising a glass)* "When Evarra and I arrived on this station, we had nothing but a tin of ration cubes and a flickering holo-vid. Tonight, we feed travelers from three empires and call twenty-eight neighbors family!"
- **EVARRA:** *(Looking warmly at Jasper and the player)* "You gave me my garden, and you gave my brother his kitchen. No matter where we came from before that escape pod, this is our home forever."
- **[Visual]:** The whole station cheers around the fountain as fireflies and honeybees drift beneath the station dome.
- **Reward:** Permanent *"Family Table"* perk (all cooked meals restore 25% more health and energy) and a framed photo of Jasper, Evarra, and the player hung above the Mess Hall counter.

# Part 2 — Job, Quill, Ix'ali & Artie

**Characters Covered:** Job (Starting Romance), Quill (Starting Romance), Ix'ali (Starting Romance), and Arthur "Artie" Vance (Unlockable Romance)

## 1. Job — Heavy Miner

**Arc Theme:** Carrying the weight of keeping the station supplied with worn-out tools, opening up beneath a quiet exterior, and sharing roots that stretch back to a homeland he has never seen.

### 2-Heart Event: "Gumbo in the Breakroom"

**Trigger:** Enter the Mines entrance breakroom between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM at 2 Hearts.

**Setting:** A small metal table near the mine lift. Job is carefully stirring a thermos of spiced stew while polishing a dented plasma pickaxe.

- **JOB:** *(Warm, unhurried Louisiana cadence)* "Well now, look who came down from the bright lights. Pull up a crate, sha—you look like you been swinging a pick all afternoon."
- **[Visual]:** Job pours a mug of rich, smoky stew and slides it across the table.
- **JOB:** "My granddaddy passed down that spice blend from old Louisiana—or so the family logbooks say. Never set foot on Earth myself, never seen a bayou or heard a cicada, yet somehow the smell of cayenne and smoked root still feels like home."
- **PLAYER CHOICE:**
  - *Option A:* "Home is wherever you bring your people and your recipes." (+High Friendship)
  - *Option B:* "This is the best thing I've tasted since landing here." (+Friendship)
- **JOB:** *(Chuckles softly, shoulders relaxing)* "Careful saying things like that, or Jasper's gonna try to steal my thermos."

### 4-Heart Event: "Cave-In on Shaft Four"

**Trigger:** Descend to Mine Level 3 after reaching 4 Hearts with Job.

**Setting:** A narrow iron-vein tunnel. Loose shale rumbles down, pinning Zaas's diving-bell crate and cracking Job's old drill rig.

- **[Visual]:** Job braces a heavy steel support beam with both arms, holding the ceiling steady while the player clears the rubble and pulls Zaas's gear free.
- **JOB:** *(Catching his breath once the brace is wedged)* "Appreciate the quick hands. Truth is, this old Terran drill's held together by prayer and copper wire. Everybody counts on my ore quotas to patch the hull, and I hate letting folks down."
- **Gameplay Unlock:** Unlocks the *"Reinforced Mining Rig"* quest with Tikka, boosting daily station ore yields.

### 6-Heart Event: "Lanterns by the Cavern Sea"

**Trigger:** Visit the Deep Caverns after awakening *The Harbinger of Destruction* and reaching 6 Hearts.

**Setting:** The glowing shore of the newly exposed subterranean ocean. Job sits on a smooth basalt boulder watching bioluminescent ripples.

- **JOB:** "All those years Zaas was hollering about underground oceans, I thought the poor fella had breathed too much rock dust. Turns out... sometimes the wildest dreams are right underneath your boots."
- **JOB:** *(Looks over with a gentle smile)* "Reminded me of the old stories about the Gulf coast back on Earth. Funny how a dry moon can surprise you when someone takes the time to dig deep."

### 8-Heart Event: "Quiet Ground" (Romance Confession)

**Trigger:** Visit the Hot Springs at dusk at 8 Hearts (backup before the Harbinger: the Engineering break room). Perk, whether or not you accept: Bedrock Strength (mining uses 20% less energy, and more geodes drop).

**Setting:** Steaming mineral pools illuminated by warm lanterns.

- **JOB:** "I'm a simple man, sha. Give me honest work, good company, and a quiet place to sit at the end of a shift. Since your ship fell through the moon's roof into that old drone's cave, every single one of those things got brighter."
- **JOB:** *(Reaches out a calloused hand, voice deep and earnest)* "I don't need to travel to Earth to find where I belong. Right here with you is all the home I'll ever want. What do you say?"
- **PLAYER CHOICE:** Accept confession or stay close friends.

### 10-Heart Event: "Bayou Under the Stars"

**Trigger:** Enter the Subterranean Ocean dock at night at 10 Hearts as partners.

**Scene:** Job builds a small wooden skiff with Tobbin's help and takes you rowing across the glowing cavern lake under crystal stalactites. **Keepsake: a carved oar charm (Claude). Backup before the Harbinger: the Shuttle Bay windows at night, where he shows you the skiff he's building.**

## 2. Quill — Medical Assistant

**Arc Theme:** Overcoming self-doubt as Maren's quiet apprentice, discovering confidence in xenobotanical medicine, and finding his voice.

### 2-Heart Event: "The Spilled Apothecary"

**Trigger:** Enter the East Wing Medbay between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM at 2 Hearts.

**Setting:** Medbay lab bench. Quill is kneeling on the floor frantically gathering scattered glass vials, his green-blue cheek blotches flushing bright teal.

- **QUILL:** *(Pushing his glasses up nervously)* "Oh, no, no—don't step there! That's luminescent cavern spore extract! If it stains Maren's clean tiles, I'll be inventorying gauze pads until Year 405!"
- **[Visual]:** The player kneels down and helps sort the intact vials into the rack.
- **QUILL:** *(Exhales gratefully)* "Thank you... Maren's treated soldiers and spacers across three sectors, and I still drop my own sample trays. Sometimes I wonder if I'm cut out to be a real medic."
- **PLAYER CHOICE:**
  - *Option A:* "You're researching cures nobody else on the station understands." (+High Friendship)
  - *Option B:* "Even Maren had to start somewhere. Keep at it." (+Friendship)

### 4-Heart Event: "Field Notes in the Dark"

**Trigger:** Enter the upper Caverns at 4 Hearts.

**Setting:** Near a cluster of glowing blue moss. Quill is sketching flora in a leather notebook while Vzz'ket stands guard nearby.

- **QUILL:** "Look at the cellular regeneration on this cavern lichen! Mixed with Merrow's hydroponic aloe, it heals burn trauma twice as fast as standard Terran medpacks—and works safely on Zaa'sh, Arachnay, and humans alike!"
- **Gameplay Unlock:** Unlocks the *"Xeno-Salve"* crafting recipe (restores full health and cures status debuffs).

### 6-Heart Event: "Doctor's Orders"

**Trigger:** Enter the Medbay in the afternoon at 6 Hearts.

**Setting:** Maren is away tending to a visiting freighter crew; Quill calmly treats a bruised miner using his own herbal salve without a single tremor.

- **MAREN (Entering quietly at the doorway, smiling proudly):** "Well done, Quill. Steady hands, sharp diagnosis. I think it's high time we put your name on the clinic door beside mine."
- **QUILL:** *(Beaming, adjusting his glasses with quiet pride)* "I... I didn't even drop a single vial."

### 8-Heart Event: "Heartbeat" (Romance Confession)

**Trigger:** Visit the Library / Medbay garden bench in the evening at 8 Hearts. Perk, whether or not you accept: Guardian Medic (+20 max health, and collapsing costs no Scrip).

**Setting:** Quiet evening light beside potted luminescent ferns.

- **QUILL:** "I've spent years studying biology—how hearts beat, how cells mend, how different species adapt to new worlds. Yet every time you walk into the Medbay, my own pulse forgets every textbook rule I've ever memorized."
- **QUILL:** *(Blushing deeply, offering a pressed glowing fern bookmark)* "You helped me believe in myself. Would you... let me care for you, not just as your medic, but as someone who loves you?"
- **PLAYER CHOICE:** Accept confession or remain close friends.

### 10-Heart Event: "Constellation Study"

**Trigger:** Visit the Observation Lounge at night at 10 Hearts as partners.

**Scene:** Quill names a newly charted bioluminescent plant species after you and gifts you an engraved brass stethoscope-compass charm. **Keepsake: the stethoscope-compass charm.**

  - *Option A:* "True strength is always about who you protect." (+High Friendship)

### 10-Heart Event: "Forged in Starlight"

**Trigger:** Enter the Top-Deck Observation Lounge at night at 10 Hearts as partners.

**Scene:** Ix'ali unveils matching ceremonial armor pauldrons engraved with the Echo Landing crest and watches the stars with you. **Keepsake: a ceremonial pauldron.**

Now Owen tells me my hometown is a historical footnote and my neighbors are seven-foot lizard clerics and fox chefs."

# Part 3 — Ana, Stu & Freya

**Characters Covered:** Anastasia "Ana" (Unlockable Romance), Stowaway Stu (Unlockable Romance), and Freya (Starting Romance)

**Structure:** 2-Heart, 4-Heart, 6-Heart, 8-Heart (Confession / Commitment), and 10-Heart (Partner Milestone) playable events.

## 1. Anastasia "Ana" — Former Pirate Heir & Flight Navigator

**Arc Theme:** Shedding the lie that fear equals loyalty, forgiving herself for her brother's crimes, and discovering what a real community looks like.

### 2-Heart Event: "No Brig, Just Soup"

**Trigger:** Visit the East Wing Medbay after rescuing Ana from her downed ship and reaching 2 Hearts.

**Setting:** Medbay recovery bay. Ana sits stiffly on the edge of a cot in a torn flight jacket, eyeing the door like a cornered wolf.

- **[Visual]:** Saffi enters carrying a steaming bowl of vegetable broth and fresh rolls, setting them gently on the tray table beside Ana.
- **ANA:** *(Tensing, voice guarded)* "If this is an interrogation tactic, save it. Ivan's dead, the Chernobog lieutenants scattered, and I'm not giving you any old stash codes."
- **SAFFI:** *(Warmly, ears tilting softly)* "It's carrot-ginger soup, sweetheart. Maren says your ribs are still bruised from the crash. Eat while it's hot."
- **[Visual]:** Saffi leaves. Ana stares at the bowl in disbelief, then looks up as the player steps closer.
- **ANA:** "I spent my whole life believing the Chernobog Clan was the only family out here—that we raided to protect our own. Then my own second-in-command shot me in the back the second I ordered them not to fire on a civilian freighter. Why did you pull me out of that wreck?"
- **PLAYER CHOICE:**
  - *Option A:* "You tried to protect innocent people. That counts." (+Friendship)
  - *Option B:* "Nobody gets left behind near Echo Landing." (+Friendship)
  - *Option C:* "I figured you owed me a rematch." (Ana lets out a surprised, dry laugh)
- **ANA:** *(Picks up the spoon quietly)* "Don't expect me to start baking pies tomorrow. Still... thank you."

### 4-Heart Event: "Grease & Second Chances"

**Trigger:** Enter the Shuttle Bay between 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM after reaching 4 Hearts with Ana.

**Setting:** Docking Deck. Ana and Freya are crouched beneath the player's ship, recalibrating thruster manifolds.

- **[Visual]:** Freya hands Ana a torque wrench; Ana adjusts a fuel injector with practiced speed.
- **FREYA:** "Whoa—where did you learn to bypass a Terran stabilizer like that? Even Tikka takes twenty minutes on those valves!"
- **ANA:** *(Wiping grease from her cheek, a rare small smile showing)* "When your engines run on stolen parts and hope, you learn to fix them blindfolded. Honestly... it feels good fixing a ship instead of patching bullet holes."
- **[Visual]:** Garrick enters the bay, pausing with arms crossed. Ana immediately stiffens, stepping back from the tools.
- **GARRICK:** "Relax, Ana. I'm not here to arrest you. Freya told me you've been charting the old Chernobog ambush corridors so Eshu's mail skiffs can avoid them. That saved two freighters this week."
- **ANA:** *(Quietly, meeting his gaze)* "I can't undo what Ivan did. I'm just trying to balance the ledger."
- **GARRICK:** *(Nods once)* "Keep doing what you're doing. You're earning your berth."

### 6-Heart Event: "Ghost of the Crown"

**Trigger:** Enter Rusk's Bar after 8:00 PM at 6 Hearts.

**Setting:** A quiet corner booth at Rusk's Bar. Ana stares at a tarnished brass compass—the only keepsake from her childhood.

- **ANA:** "Ivan wasn't always a monster, you know. When our parents died on a frontier mining rig, he stole food so I wouldn't starve. Somewhere along the way, he started believing his own ridiculous fairy tales about being a king—and I was too scared of being alone to tell him no."
- **ANA:** "Look at this place. Rusk pouring drinks for miners, Freya lending me her spare jackets, you checking in on me without asking for a thing. For the first time in my life, I'm not looking over my shoulder."
- **PLAYER CHOICE:**
  - *Option A:* "You belong here, Ana—past and all." (+High Romance/Friendship)
  - *Option B:* "You chose who you wanted to be when it mattered." (+High Romance/Friendship)

### 8-Heart Event: "Co-Pilot's Promise" (Romance Confession)

**Trigger:** Visit the Top-Deck Observation Lounge at night at 8 Hearts. Perk, whether or not you accept: Wingmate (+20% ship evasion and faster FTL travel).

**Setting:** Under the panoramic glass dome, watching meteors streak across the Neutral Zone.

- **ANA:** "I used to think freedom meant flying solo with nobody able to catch you. Turns out, flying solo is just lonely."
- **ANA:** *(Turns to the player, cheeks faintly flushed, voice steady)* "You saved my life twice—once in that wreckage, and once by giving me a reason to stay on Echo Landing. If you still want a navigator by your side... I don't want to go anywhere without you."
- **PLAYER CHOICE:** Accept confession (unlocks romantic status and co-pilot ship buffs) or remain close friends.

### 10-Heart Event: "True North"

**Trigger:** Board your ship in the Shuttle Bay at sunset after reaching 10 Hearts as partners.

**Scene:** Ana surprises the player with a custom-calibrated star-chart console etched with both of their initials and takes you on a quiet scenic orbit above the moon's glowing crater. **Keepsake: the star-chart console and a custom flight jacket.**

## 2. Stowaway Stu — Hotel Lobby Boy & Former Drifter

**Species: Moogli. Arrives on the first transport of visitors after the pirate base raid, hidden in a hydroponics crate.**

**Arc Theme:** Running away from a forgotten past, learning that belonging isn't a trap, and taking pride in building a home for others.

### 2-Heart Event: "Packed Bags by the Airlock"

**Trigger:** Enter the Echo Landing Hotel Lobby in the morning at 2 Hearts.

**Setting:** The newly restored Hotel Lobby. Stu is polishing the brass reception bell while a small, worn canvas duffel bag sits hidden behind the potted fern.

- **STU:** *(Jumping slightly when the player walks in, grinning sheepishly)* "Oh! Hey, Boss! Just, uh, testing the acoustics on the lobby bell! Crisp, right? Very Parisian, just like Sara ordered!"
- **S.A.R.A. (Holo-Emitter):** "Indeed, Stu. Though I do wonder why your duffel bag is packed and sitting by the terminal airlock."
