Resident side-walk fix (21 Nov 2026)

All 31 resident walk sheets (art/npc_<id>.png), left and right walks only.

Legs. Contact strides (rows 2 and 3): the thin stick legs are repainted as solid legs five
pixels wide (four on the smallest legs), cut from each resident's own standing legs: one steps
three pixels forward and the other three back from the hip, the far one in shade. Each leg
runs straight from its hip, and its foot sits under its own ankle, so the foot always
travels with the leg. The body drops one pixel. Passing poses (rows 4 and 5): the standing
legs, with the lifted foot hidden behind the planted one. Tails, robes and bags follow the
standing pose. The Arachnay (Sesska, Eshu, Tikka)
keep their painted many-legged strides; only their passing poses changed.

Arms. Every resident except the Arachnay swings the near arm from the shoulder: five pixels
forward or four back on the strides, two on the passing steps. No far hand is drawn.
- Evarra and Jasper use rig parts (see below): an arm sprite cut or drawn separately from the
  body, so nothing is left behind when it swings.
- Ana (both uniforms), Artie, Freya, Garrick, Ix'ali, Job, Maren (weekend), Owen, Stu and Zaas
  swing their own painted arm, outlined against the body.
- The others get a sleeve drawn over the body in their cloth colours with a mitten hand.
  These are the ones to move to rig parts next.

Rig parts. art/rig/npc_<id>_side.png holds two 24 x 48 cells facing left: the body (head,
torso, tail and standing legs, with the torso painted in where the arm hangs) and the near
arm at rest, in the same registration. npc_<id>_side.txt gives the shoulder pivot, the top
row of the hand and the hip row. tools/rig_walk.py builds the four side-walk rows from them;
tools/rig_parts.py cuts or draws the parts. Repainting a part in a pixel editor and running
rig_walk.py again updates the walk.

Merrow: four pixels taller (37 instead of 33), now taller than Bram and Tobbin. Rows of her
overalls through the chest, waist and upper legs are repeated with her feet fixed, in every
cell of npc_merrow.png (all four directions) and npc_merrow_act.png.

Garrick: his jaw in profile steps back over its last rows, in the walk and in the
left-facing drink pose of npc_garrick_act.png.

Standing frames (except Garrick's chin and Merrow's height), front and back walks and all other activity rows are
unchanged. Every sheet keeps four distinct walk drawings per direction, and all of these files
are already in art/hand_edited.txt.

tools/fix_npc_walk.py rebuilds the walk rows from the standing pose; its CAST table holds each
resident's hip row and either a hand box or a shoulder point. Run tools/stretch_merrow.py on
Merrow's sheets before it and tools/fix_chin.py on Garrick after it.
tools/fix_npc_passing.py (with --no-heel) rebuilds the Arachnay's passing poses.
