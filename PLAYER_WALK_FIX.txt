Player side-walk fix (21 Nov 2026)

Left and right walks, all ten outfits on both bodies.

Contact strides (rows 2 and 3): the painted legs, head and hair are kept. The arms, which
stuck straight out from the waist with no upper arm, are redrawn: the near arm is outlined
against the jacket and swings from the shoulder, its hand six pixels ahead of the hip
(rising two) or five behind it (rising one), and the far arm swings the other way behind the
body, one shade darker. The jacket sits one pixel behind the hips under the painted head.

Passing poses (rows 4 and 5), which had collapsed legs, a stacked second boot and a buried
arm, are rebuilt from each outfit's standing profile: the planted leg keeps the standing
silhouette, the other boot lifts two pixels and tucks three behind the calf so only its heel
shows, and the arm is two pixels into its swing. The carry poses match.

Changed: art/player/{tops,bottoms,faces,hair,action_tops}(_mask).png and the same files in
art/player/body2/, plus the fallbacks art/player.png and art/player_actions.png. Front and
back views, standing frames, every action row from grip onwards, eating, watering and the
resident sheets are untouched. Right-facing frames stay exact mirrors of the left.

tools/fix_player_side_walk.py regenerates these rows from the standing frames:
    python3 tools/fix_player_side_walk.py art/player <output>/player --keep-strides
Without --keep-strides it also redraws the contact legs as shorter steps.
