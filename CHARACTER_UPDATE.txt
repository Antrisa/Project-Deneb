Echo Landing
Player and resident animation update

Installation

Apply this cumulative update to Project-Deneb's art24 checkout at 6c4345cd82d4.
Extract the folders alongside project.godot and open the project in Godot 4.
The updated files come from revision 9abf32e7214b. On a newer or independently
edited checkout, merge the changed scripts before running the project.

The cast

All 27 scheduled residents wear their Soft Indie designs while walking, eating,
and working or studying. Each walk sheet has four 24 × 48 directions. The five
rows are standing, contact A, contact B, passing A and passing B. Playback uses
rows 1, 3, 2 and 4. Sprites share a foot anchor 2 pixels above the bottom of
each frame, including the smaller children and Moogli.

Activities use four 36 × 48 frames per row. The wider cells accommodate hands
and tools. Work follows each resident's role. Freya welds, Jasper cooks, Sesska
weaves, Stu carries luggage and the children study or draw. The same character
art also appears at garden, chess, hologame and other scheduled social stops.
Artie, Stu, Tally and Ana follow their regular routines after their arrivals.
Ooma visits on Wednesday and Saturday and breaks for supper during her visit.
Jasper and Evarra take turns eating after kitchen service. The alternate
outfits and event poses match the redesign.

S.A.R.A. appears at the station projectors. All 33 of her gestures and
projection clips use her updated hologram. The summoned screen, teacup,
flower and other object layers retain their own timing. See sara/README.md
and sara/SARA_Animation_Sampler.gif.

Player

Both character creator models and their customizable layers are included,
along with the earlier player action updates. The watering can stays in front
of the chest on the pour, with the near hand on the handle and a level head.
See docs/ART_HANDOFF_CHARACTERS.md for the native layouts and player poses.

Preview and verify

The previews folder shows the in-game art at integer scale. It contains the
lineup, the four-direction walks, and a side-by-side cast view of work and meals.
Project tests can be run from the game directory.

    godot --headless --editor --import --quit
    godot --headless --script res://tests/resident_day_test.gd
    godot --headless --script res://tests/walk_cycle_test.gd
    godot --headless --script res://tests/social_test.gd
    godot --headless --script res://sara/test_animations.gd
    godot --headless --script res://tests/player_model_test.gd

The full-day test starts on a Wednesday after both rescues. It checks that each
resident actually plays all four phases of walking, a meal, and a role activity
during their ordinary schedule.
