# Echo Landing — Act I Prologue & Game Opening Script

> Copied into the repo on 22 Nov 2026 from Zachary's Drive doc "Echo Landing - Game Opening & Prologue Script" (last edited 18 Nov), text unchanged. Review notes are in `STORY_ARCS.md`. Until Zachary says otherwise, the Drive doc is the source of truth and this is a copy.

**Project:** Project Orion: Echo Landing

**Sequence:** Playable Prologue & Opening Cutscenes (Scenes 1–4)

**Purpose:** Establishes the Neutral Zone setting, introduces Ivan the Rabble as the early-game antagonist, triggers the Starborn Mother-Drone lexicon upload, and unlocks the Precursor Reliquary restoration mechanic.

## Scene 1: The Ambush in the Dark

**Setting:** Outer Neutral Zone — Asteroid Belt Sector 7. Deep space bathed in faint violet nebula light.

**Visuals:** Pixel-art cockpit view transitioning to exterior ship view. The player's rookie bounty-hunter corvette, the *Wandering Star*, drifts cautiously past jagged iron asteroids. Red proximity alerts blink across the console.

- **[SFX: Low radar ping. Hull creaks.]**
- **SHIP COMPUTER:** *Warning. Unregistered transponders emerging from asteroid shadow. Three—correction, four vessels. Weapons hot.*
- **[Visual]:** A massive, asymmetrical pirate flagship—patched together from stolen Terran hull plates, Zaa'sh armor slabs, and gaudy gold trim—looms out of the dust. Across its bow is painted *THE RABBLE'S CROWN*.
- **[SFX: Incoming hail static.]**
- **IVAN THE RABBLE (Holo-Screen):** *(Booming, theatrical, draped in a coat stitched from medals and velvet curtains)* "Halt, trespasser! You drift in the royal hunting grounds of the Chernobog Clan! Bow before His Dread Majesty—**Ivan the Rabble**, rightful heir to the ancient thrones of Old Earth!"
- **PIRATE LIEUTENANT (Off-screen, muffled):** "Uh, Boss... the book says 'Terrible.' Not 'Rabble.'"
- **IVAN THE RABBLE:** *(Snapping off-mic)* "The ink was smudged, you illiterate bilge-rat! 'Rabble' strikes fear into the hearts of peasants! *(Back to camera, puffing chest)* Surrender your cargo and your ship, little hunter, and I may spare you for the mines!"
- **PLAYER CHOICE (Dialogue Prompt):**
  - *Option A:* "I'm a licensed bounty hunter. Stand down!"
  - *Option B:* "You misread a history book, didn't you?"
  - *Option C:* [Divert full power to engines and run]
- **IVAN THE RABBLE:** *(Laughs darkly)* "Bold words for a minnow! Broadside them! Leave enough scrap to patch my galley!"

**Gameplay Beat (Brief Tutorial Skirmish):** Player fires two volleys before an overwhelming plasma salvo strikes the port nacelle. Alarms blare; sparks shower the cockpit.

- **SHIP COMPUTER:** *Shields offline. Hull integrity at 18%. FTL drive core destabilizing.*
- **[Visual]:** Desperate, the player slams the FTL lever without plotting coordinates. Space folds violently—a blinding flash tears the rear cargo section away as the crippled forward hull vanishes into hyperspace.

## Scene 2: The Crash & The Dying Mother-Drone

**Setting:** An uncharted, crater-pocked moon deep inside the Neutral Zone.

**Visuals:** The burning wreck of the *Wandering Star* streaks across a starry sky, slams into a shadowy crater rim, and breaks straight through a fragile crust of lunar rock into a vast, pitch-black subterranean cavern.

- **[SFX: Deafening crunch of stone and metal, settling into eerie, echoing silence. Faint dripping water.]**
- **[Visual]:** Emergency red lights flicker inside the tilted cockpit. Outside the cracked canopy, the cavern isn't empty. Half-buried in ancient basalt rests a colossal, seamless silver construct shaped like a sleeping celestial seed—an original **Precursor Mother-Drone**.
- **[SFX: A deep, resonant crystalline hum vibrates through the hull.]**
- **[Visual]:** Faint golden-violet veins flicker across the Mother-Drone's weathered carapace. A single optical lens iris opens, casting a soft beam of light onto the player's cockpit.
- **MOTHER-DRONE (Subtitle / Resonant Echo):** *...Signal... faint... Core reserves at 0.04%... Organic neural pattern detected...*
- **[Visual]:** The beam intensifies into a blinding prism of violet and gold light, washing over the player. Geometric star-glyphs swirl across the screen—incomprehensible, musical, and warm—before condensing into a tiny glowing mark at the corner of the player's vision.
- **MOTHER-DRONE:** *...Remember... the song... Tend the small... worlds...*
- **[SFX: The hum pitches downward and goes completely silent. The golden veins fade to dark silver.]**
- **[Visual]:** Darkness closes in on the cockpit—until a crisp blue radio indicator lights up on the cracked console.
- **S.A.R.A. (Voice over Comms, calm and poised):** "Unidentified vessel, this is Sara at Echo Landing. My seismic sensors just registered a rather dramatic unscheduled arrival in Sector Four. Do you require assistance?"
- **[Visual]:** The player reaches toward the mic, vision blurring, and blacks out.

## Scene 3: Waking Up on Echo Landing (Cutscene)

**Setting:** East Wing Medbay & Central Plaza Reception.

**Visuals:** Soft, warm interior lighting. The player wakes up on a clean cot in the Medbay. **Maren** is checking a datapad beside the bed while **Quill** organizes bandages nearby.

- **MAREN:** "Easy now, don't sit up too fast. You took a nasty knock when your ship decided to redecorate our lower cavern."
- **QUILL:** *(Adjusting his glasses)* "Your vitals are remarkably stable, though your neural scans showed a brief electromagnetic spike. Probably just FTL feedback. Nothing a good meal from Jasper won't cure."
- **MAREN:** "Your ship's in the Shuttle Bay—Freya and Tikka hauled the forward half up with the magnetic crane. When you're steady on your feet, head out to the plaza and speak with Sara at Reception. She's eager to meet our newest guest."

**Cutscene Beat (First Walk on the Concourse):** Maren helps the player out of the Medbay onto the Main Concourse. Sunlight lamps illuminate the central Hydroponic Fountain; residents (Merrow tending planters, Tobbin carrying plasteel beams, Pip, Nub, and Kit racing past) wave as the player walks to the central desk under the painted-over *RECEPTION* sign. A poised, warm holographic woman appears above the emitter.

- **S.A.R.A.:** "Welcome to Echo Landing! I am S.A.R.A.—though everyone here simply calls me Sara. I apologize for the somewhat rustic accommodations; we are still transitioning from 'abandoned Terran listening post' to 'boutique lunar sanctuary.'"
- **S.A.R.A.:** "Freya tells me your ship needs heavy hull plating and a new FTL regulator before it can fly again. Until then, you will stay in the old barracks in the Spaceport District, which I hope will one day be our hotel. I would like you quarantined there for a few days, just to be safe. Feel free to explore, meet your neighbors, and—oh! Before I forget, our resident scholar, Tar'Besh, asked to see you in the Survey Suite upstairs. He seemed unusually excited about the cavern your ship broke into."

## Scene 4: The Reliquary & The Violet Surge

**Setting:** Top Deck — Survey Suite, Museum & Inner Sanctum.

**Visuals:** Panoramic glass dome overlooking the starry Neutral Zone. Glass display cases hold Terran dog tags, Zaa'sh crests, and ancient fossils. At the back, heavy blast doors lead into a quiet, blue-lit chamber holding three pedestals with inert, crystalline vessels.

- **TAR'BESH:** *(Smoothing his tattered purple-and-gold robes, amber eyes wide)* "Ah, the sky-faller! Remarkable. When we pulled your hull from the lower shaft, I descended to inspect the breach—and found the dormant shell of a prime Sky-Weaver vessel! For twenty years I have searched this moon for signs of the ancients, and you literally dropped through their roof."
- **TAR'BESH:** *(Gesturing to the three crystals in the Inner Sanctum)* "These three vessels were recovered from the deep crust years ago. Inert. Silent. No Zaa'sh tool or Terran scanner has ever woken them. Yet look—since you entered this room, their facets have begun to shimmer."
- **[SFX: A loud metallic CLANG from the outer museum hall, followed by guilty giggling.]**
- **NUB (Off-screen):** "Pip, run! The shiny helmet fell off the stand!"
- **TAR'BESH:** *(Crest flaring in exasperation)* "By the stars—*children!* That is a Third-Dynasty ceremonial crest, not a bowl for moon-pebbles! Stay right here, traveler; I will return in a moment!"
- **[Visual]:** Tar'Besh hurries out into the outer hall. Alone in the Sanctum, the strange glyphs from the Mother-Drone pulse softly at the edge of the player's vision, resonating in harmony with the three crystals.
- **[Gameplay Prompt]:** *Approach the central pedestal and touch the crystal.*
- **[SFX: A deep harmonic chord builds—then erupts in a resonant chime.]**
- **[Visual]:** A brilliant **violet wave of light** surges from the crystal, enveloping the player. The unreadable glyphs in the player's mind untangle into clear, glowing words. Inside the three vessels, swirling ribbons of luminous nano-fluid wake up and swirl to life.
- **THE CHANCELLOR OF HEARTHS (Amber-Gold Crystal, warm and fussy):** *"Oh, my stars—is someone finally awake out there? Look at the dust on these pedestals! Dreadful hospitality!"*
- **THE SOVEREIGN OF HYDRO-FLORA (Emerald-Teal Crystal, serene and poetic):** *"Peace, Chancellor. A mind tuned to the Mother-Drone's song stands before us. Tell me, small walker—does green life still breathe beneath this stone sky?"*
- **THE FORGEMASTER OF KEELS (Cobalt-Silver Crystal, gruff):** *"Never mind the poetry. This station's hull is held together with rust and hope. Bring us raw ore and materials, Archivist, and our nano-fluid will weave this place back into proper shape."*
- **TAR'BESH:** *(Rushing back in, stopping dead in the doorway as the three crystals glow brightly)* "Impossible... they speak?! Wait—keep your voice down! If word reaches Zaa'sh space that sentient constructs are active here, the Junta will send warships to destroy them. Let this remain our secret for now, friend. Together, we will rebuild Echo Landing—one crystal at a time."

**End of Prologue:** Unlocks the *Precursor Reliquary* menu tab, the first three Custodian Restoration Bundles, and free exploration of Echo Landing. The screen fades, and play begins the next morning with the player waking in the head officer's quarters of the old barracks (the future hotel).
